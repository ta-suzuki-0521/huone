Huone Publishing

HUONE is a publishing project by copywriter Naoki Karikawa and Art Director/ designer Takuro Suzuki of Nippon Design Center. "HUONE" means "room" in Finnish. Through its books, the project explores personal truths and quiet questions about society,
as if in conversation inside a private room.
